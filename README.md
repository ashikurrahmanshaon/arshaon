# ARSHAON.COM — Interactive 3D Workspace & Editorial Portfolio

**Owner:** Ashikur Rahman Shaon (`ARSHAON`)  
**Domain:** [https://arshaon.com](https://arshaon.com)

An architectural, editorial personal website and interactive 3D executive workspace built with **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber**, **Drei**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Zustand**.

---

## Five Core Experiences

1. **A. The Entrance (`#entrance`)**: Cinematic editorial hero introducing **Ashikur Rahman Shaon** (`Welcome to my digital world.`) with an architectural workspace preview and the **Explore My World** call-to-action.
2. **B. My Workspace (`#workspace`)**: One realistic, sunlit 3D architectural office environment featuring a sculpted walnut desk, computer workstation, dual studio monitors, mechanical keyboard, wireless mouse, cognac-leather executive chair, built-in walnut & travertine bookshelf, brass task lamp, and a multifunction CV printer.
   - **Computer / Monitor** → Opens Portfolio & Creations preview.
   - **Printer** → Opens A4 CV Preview, PDF download & browser print.
   - **Bookshelf** → Opens Biography & Skills overview.
3. **C. About Me (`#about`)**: Editorial biography featuring Introduction, Professional Identity (`ARSHAON`), Skills, Career Journey, Personal Interests, and Executive Practice Areas.
4. **D. My Creations (`#creations`)**: Curated showcase across **Websites**, **SaaS Products**, **AI Projects**, **Digital Marketing Campaigns**, and **Creative Work**, with interactive project previews.
5. **E. My CV & Contact (`#cv-contact`)**: Inline A4 CV preview, direct `.pdf` download, print-isolated browser printing (`window.print()`), direct email (`contact@arshaon.com`), LinkedIn/social profiles, and a collaboration inquiry form.

---

## Editing Content

All personal, portfolio, and resume data is stored in clean, editable TypeScript files:

- [src/data/profileData.ts](./src/data/profileData.ts) — Introduction, professional identity, career history, skills, interests, and personal journey.
- [src/data/businessData.ts](./src/data/businessData.ts) — Executive practice areas & capabilities.
- [src/data/projectsData.ts](./src/data/projectsData.ts) — Creations across Websites, SaaS Products, AI Projects, Digital Marketing Campaigns, and Creative Work.
- [src/data/cvData.ts](./src/data/cvData.ts) — Print-ready A4 CV sections.
